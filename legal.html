2. Privacy policy

What we collect

Lumen does not require an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal details. We do not run analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.

Everything Lumen remembers about you (your saved mods and your display preferences) is stored only in your own browser, using localStorage. It is never sent to us. We cannot see it, and you can delete it at any time (see Data deletion).

What the hosting and third-party services can see

When your browser loads the site or calls a service listed under Third-party services, that service receives standard connection data, including your IP address, browser type, and the page or file requested. We do not control those logs, but each provider's own privacy policy applies to them.

Why we process it

The only processing is what your browser does to show you Modrinth content, which is necessary to provide the service you asked for.

Children

Lumen is a general-audience site and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), because we collect none. If you believe a child has given us personal information through a contact channel, contact us at [contact email] and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data, or to object to its use. Because we hold no personal data on our servers, most of these requests are answered by using the controls in Data deletion. For anything else, contact [contact email]. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

5. Cookie policy

Lumen does not set cookies. It does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking cookies.

Lumen stores two items in your browser's localStorage, which is separate from cookies and is never sent to our servers:

KeyWhat it holdsWhy
lumen-settingsYour theme, accent color, density, motion, and page-size choicesSo your preferences are remembered
lumen-savedThe mods you saved (title, slug, icon URL, and basic stats)So your saved list is kept

These are strictly necessary for the features you choose to use, so we do not show a cookie banner. You can remove them at any time in Settings → Delete all my data, or by clearing your browser's site data. If we ever add cookies or non-essential tracking, we will add a consent banner first.

6. Data deletion
